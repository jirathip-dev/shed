`shed account add|ls|login|status|rm|use`

The accounts shed keeps on this host: `add <provider> <name> [--folder DIR] [--token-file FILE]` makes one (or adopts a folder), `login` runs its login on a redacted screen (type the code), `login --token` its setup-token command, saving the token it prints for runs on other machines, `status` reads it now, `ls` lists them (the preferred one marked) and `rm` signs one out and removes it (docs/accounts/store.md). `use <provider> <name>` makes a ready named account the one `auto` picks first and moves every live `auto` and `default` lane to it; `use <provider> --clear` drops the preference (docs/accounts/accounts.md).
